WHY SHOULD YOU WATCH SOUTH PARK?

(OR SOUTH AMERICA, WHATEVER.)

WHAT IS SOUTH PARK?

Set in the fictional town of South Park, Colorado, the series follows four boys, Stan, Kyle, Cartman and Kenny, as they deal with school, friendship and the weirdness of the world around them. Back in the 1990s, Trey Parker and Matt Stone created a short animated film using construction-paper cutouts. Their early work eventually led to South Park, which premiered in 1997. Today, the show is known for its simple animation style, distinctive voices, and stories that can go from normal to completely ridiculous in seconds.

FIVE REASONS TO WATCH SOUTH PARK

  1. Cartman is a terrible person, and somehow that's exactly what makes him entertaining.
  2. It has a unique animation style that sets it apart from other shows.
  3. You never know what is going to happen next. One minute it's an ordinary day, the next it's complete chaos.
  4. The episodes may look ridiculous, but they often reflect real problems in the world.
  5. It's not just about the jokes. The characters, their ridiculous decisions and their chaotic friendships make every episode memorable.

CARTMAN

Selfish, manipulative and always convinced he's right. Nobody causes chaos quite like Cartman.

BUTTERS

Sweet, innocent and incredibly unlucky. Things just never seem to go Butters' way.

TWEEK

Anxiety in human form. Always stressed, constantly panicking, and basically running on coffee.

WENDY

Smart, confident and outspoken. A feminist who isn't afraid to stand up for what she believes in.

NOT FOR EVERYONE!

  • A lot of swearing. A LOT.
  • Some jokes are definitely questionable.
  • Politics, religion, stereotypes and pretty much anything else can become a target.
  • Expect some weird and occasionally disturbing scenes.
  • Not every joke will land, and not everyone will find the show funny.
